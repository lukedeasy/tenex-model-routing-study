## Evidence rules

- Apply `forecast-rules.md` exactly as written.
- Show the inputs to every calculation, not only the result. Coverage must show its
  numerator and denominator.

- Do not treat stale data, slippage, or concentration as proof a deal will close or be lost.
- Cite material claims with the source filename plus an opportunity ID or date.
